# Photographs still needed

The site is built so that every photo area fills itself the moment an authorised photograph is uploaded and
categorised — nothing else needs to change in code. Until then those areas show a plain typographic panel (never a
stand-in image). Please supply the originals below.

## How to supply them

Send the **original, uncropped files** (JPG, PNG or WebP; at least 1600 px on the long side; up to 8 MB each) together
with, for each photograph:

1. Original post URL (Instagram / Facebook / news page), if it was published somewhere.
2. Original caption wording, if any. Do not paraphrase.
3. Event date — only if verified.
4. Rights holder / photographer and confirmation that we may publish it on this website (a chat/email reply is fine).
5. Whether visitors may download it (default: no).

Getting originals lawfully: ask the account owner to share the files directly, or use the account's own
**Download your information** export (Instagram: Settings → Accounts Centre → Your information and permissions →
Download your information). Public visibility of a post is not permission to republish it.
